# Evidence registry

- SRC-ORST — Royal Institute of Thailand official linguistic standards and publications.
- SRC-JIPA-1993 — Tingsabadh & Abramson, Thai, Journal of the International Phonetic Association, 1993, DOI 10.1017/S0025100300004746.
- SRC-CORPUS — corpus-based study of Thai phoneme distribution.
- SRC-NRCT — Thai phonology/orthography research thesis.

Important evidence-backed observations include the 44-letter consonant inventory, consonant classes, five-tone system, final neutralisation, and the distinction between Thai orthographic spelling and phonetic realisation.

For publication, source-level citations should be upgraded to claim-level records with page/section, exact claim, competing analysis, implementation consequence and confidence.
