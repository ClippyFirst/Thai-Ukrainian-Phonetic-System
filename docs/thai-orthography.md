# Thai orthography

Thai vowel notation is distributed around consonants and may be preposed in logical Unicode order. Therefore raw character order and linguistic constituent order are separate representations.

The traditional consonant inventory contains 44 letters, including obsolete ฃ and ฅ. Their class membership is synchronically relevant to tone spelling.

Important future parser domains include preposed vowels, multi-mark vowel patterns, implicit vowels, silent letters, leading ห นำ, อ as carrier, รร and other lexicalized orthographic patterns.

The system must preserve Unicode provenance when deriving linguistic structure.
